The story, in your hands.

Two published books. One connected journey through pressure, identity and rebuilding. Still Here is the forthcoming third book.

01 / Published / Memoir

The Pub, The Punch and The Path

From the street to the soul.

From Narooma to London's high-pressure pubs and onward to Bali, Mark tells the story of the environments that shaped him, the cost of always being ready for a fight, and the search for another way to live.

For readers drawn to candid memoirs about hospitality, identity, survival and starting again.

02 / Published / Lived experience

Bipolar Aware

A New Management System.

Mark's personal reflections on living with bipolar disorder, noticing patterns and finding language for his experience. It explores awareness, identity and the role of practical routines in his own life.

A personal perspective, not a clinical treatment guide or a replacement for professional care.
