The Business

Sunshooters Bar & Grill is a sports bar and grill located in Legian, Bali, operating with an 11-person team. It is a full-service hospitality venue running food delivery through GoFood and GrabFood, managing a live-sports calendar across multiple time zones (NRL, AFL, UFC, EPL, NBA), and operating in one of the most competitive F&B strips in Bali.

Mark Tulloch, founder of Lumindra, operates Sunshooters directly. This means every system built for the bar has to survive real business pressure — not just look good in a proposal.

"Most consultants advise from the sidelines. I built Lumindra's systems inside a live venue where they had to actually work. That is what makes the advice different."

— Mark Tulloch, Founder of Lumindra

The Challenges

Like most Bali hospitality businesses, the pressure points were real and expensive.

Operational friction

  • Inconsistent team communication
  • Manual order tracking across delivery platforms
  • Heavy admin load on the owner
  • Reactive decision-making instead of proactive management
  • No single view of daily business performance

Visibility & marketing gaps

  • Inconsistent digital presence across platforms
  • Manual content creation consuming significant time
  • Delivery platform listings not optimised
  • No structured customer retention system
  • Sports schedule communication disjointed

The Lumindra Approach

Rather than adding complexity, the focus was on clarity and practical systems that staff could actually use.

AI & Automation layer

  • WhatsApp Business AI agent built and deployed
  • Nine quick-reply commands: /menu, /happy, /book, /sport, /order, /smoothie, /villa, /where, /direct
  • Automated greeting and away message flows
  • AI-assisted content planning and scheduling
  • Delivery platform optimisation and menu audit

Operations Command Centre

  • Custom dashboard pulling real-time POS, financials, stock, and roster data
  • Daily AI summary and three priority actions
  • Staff operations guide built as a reusable template
  • Structured morning briefing process
  • Task tracking, maintenance log, marketing pipeline in Notion

Every element was designed to reduce the owner's cognitive load and create consistent daily execution without hiring a management layer.

What This Proves in Practice

Working inside a live venue reinforces a set of core truths that no amount of theory can replicate.

Clarity beats complexity

Most businesses do not need more tools. They need better structure. The biggest wins at Sunshooters came from simplification, not addition.

AI is only useful when tied to real workflow

The value is not in the software itself. It is in how it reduces friction, eliminates repeated manual work, and improves consistency under pressure.

The founder's state affects the system

When the leader is overloaded, the business becomes reactive. Founder clarity and business clarity are inseparable — which is why Conscious Systems runs alongside the operational work.

Small changes compound fast

Better communication, tighter routines, stronger content, and clearer decisions each create modest improvement individually. Together, they create measurable lift across the business.
