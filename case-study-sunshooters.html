The business

Sunshooters Bar & Grill

A sports bar and grill in Kerobokan, Bali, with an 11-person team. A full-service hospitality venue running food delivery through GoFood and GrabFood, managing a live-sports calendar across multiple time zones (NRL, AFL, UFC, EPL, NBA), in one of the most competitive F&B strips in Bali.

Mark Tulloch operates Sunshooters directly. Every system built for the bar has to survive real business pressure, not just look good in a proposal.

Most consultants advise from the sidelines. I built Lumindra's systems inside a live venue where they had to actually work. That is what makes the advice different.Mark Tulloch, Founder of Lumindra
The challenges

The pressure points were real and expensive.

Operational friction

  • Inconsistent team communication
  • Manual order tracking across delivery platforms
  • Heavy admin load on the owner
  • Reactive decision-making instead of proactive management
  • No single view of daily business performance

Visibility & marketing gaps

  • Inconsistent digital presence across platforms
  • Manual content creation consuming significant time
  • Delivery platform listings not optimised
  • No structured customer retention system
  • Sports schedule communication disjointed
The Lumindra approach

Clarity and practical systems.

Rather than adding complexity, the focus was on systems that staff could actually use.

AI & automation layer

  • WhatsApp Business AI agent built and deployed
  • Nine quick-reply commands: /menu, /happy, /book, /sport, /order, /smoothie, /villa, /where, /direct
  • Automated greeting and away message flows
  • AI-assisted content planning and scheduling
  • Delivery platform optimisation and menu audit

Operations command centre

  • Custom dashboard pulling real-time POS, financials, stock and roster data
  • Daily AI summary and three priority actions
  • Staff operations guide built as a reusable template
  • Structured morning briefing process
  • Task tracking, maintenance log, marketing pipeline in Notion

Every element was designed to reduce the owner's cognitive load and create consistent daily execution without hiring a management layer.

What this proves

In practice.

01

Clarity beats complexity

Most businesses do not need more tools. They need better structure. The biggest wins came from simplification, not addition.

02

AI needs real workflow

The value is in how it reduces friction, eliminates repeated manual work and improves consistency under pressure.

03

Founder state matters

When the leader is overloaded, the business becomes reactive. Founder clarity and business clarity are inseparable.

04

Small changes compound

Better communication, tighter routines, stronger content and clearer decisions together create measurable lift.
