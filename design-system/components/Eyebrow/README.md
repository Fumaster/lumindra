# Eyebrow

Small uppercase pill labels in three sizes: `eyebrow` above a hero or section heading, `offer-tag` at the top of an offer card, `t-pill` for a row of proof words under testimonials.

**Consumer provides:** a short label. Separate two ideas with a middle dot ` · `, the house separator used everywhere in the brand's copy.

- `eyebrow` and `offer-tag` set text in `brand` on `brand-tint` with a `hairline-strong` border, `radius-pill`.
- `t-pill` is quieter: `ink-muted` text, `hairline` border, with one bold word in `brand`.
- One eyebrow per heading. Keep it under about 40 characters.
