Mark Tulloch / Conscious Systems

A lived story. A sound of its own.

Conscious Systems is the music project and label created by Mark Tulloch. Songs about pressure, identity, getting back up and finding a way forward.

The words come from Mark's own journey. The books put that journey on the page; the music gives listeners another way into it. Begin with either album, then explore the singles.
