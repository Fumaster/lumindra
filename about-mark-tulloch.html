The story

Australia. London. Bali.

Learning the room

From Narooma to hospitality venues across Australia and the UK, Mark learned to lead teams, read pressure and keep a business moving.

Building in Bali

A former COO of Bali Realty, he brought that operating experience into property, hospitality and business leadership.

Finding words for it

His books explore survival, identity and awareness. Writing songs as Conscious Systems gave him another way to tell the story, especially to men who find it difficult to talk about their own.

Putting it to work

Today he operates Sunshooters Bar & Grill in Kerobokan and builds Lumindra around practical systems and clearer decisions.

About Mark

The short biography.

Mark Tulloch is an Australian-born author, recording artist and business operator based in Bali. Originally from Narooma, he has spent more than 35 years in hospitality and operations across Australia, the United Kingdom and Indonesia, including a former role as COO of Bali Realty. He founded Lumindra and creates music as Conscious Systems. His published books, The Pub, The Punch and The Path and Bipolar Aware, explore lived experience, identity and rebuilding. His albums, Systems of Light and Your Inner Voice, carry that story into music. He operates Sunshooters Bar & Grill in Kerobokan, Bali.
