About

Real businesses. Real pressure.

Mark is the founder of Lumindra, a Bali-based AI consulting and business systems practice headquartered in Kerobokan, Indonesia. He is a former Chief Operating Officer of Bali Realty and has more than 35 years of practical experience across hospitality operations, business leadership and operational systems design.

Before founding Lumindra, Mark built his career across hospitality venues and business operations in Australia, the United Kingdom and Bali. He has managed large operational teams, led business turnarounds and worked in high-pressure environments where clarity, structure and decision-making under pressure were the daily reality.

He holds an Associate Diploma of Hospitality Management and an Advanced Certificate in Commercial Catering. He is a licensed Armed Personal Protection Agent and holds a Certificate in Positive Psychiatry. He is a published author with three titles, a trained crisis counsellor, a mental health advocate, a public speaker and a commercially licensed music creator, with two albums licensed through APRA AMCOS.

He is a founding member of the Bali AI Founders Club and runs a YouTube channel at @lumindrabali focused on practical AI for real businesses. He currently operates Sunshooters Bar & Grill in Kerobokan, Bali, a live operational laboratory where Lumindra's systems are tested before being recommended to clients.

I didn't build Lumindra to add to the noise. I built it because I've spent 35 years inside real businesses, and I know what the gap between good advice and actual implementation looks like. Lumindra closes that gap.Mark Tulloch, Founder
